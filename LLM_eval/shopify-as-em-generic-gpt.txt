### Main reservations

There are a few things I'd want to probe in an interview rather than reject over:

1. **Engineering-management depth vs. applied-science leadership.**
   The resume is excellent, but the candidate's title history is somewhat science/technology-lead oriented rather than a conventional long-tenured Engineering Manager path. At Etsy, the team was ~5 ICs, and at Vody ~11 across science, engineering, product, and ops. I'd want to establish how deeply they have managed *software/ML engineering execution* versus primarily providing technical/scientific leadership.

2. **Current hands-on engineering depth.**
   The resume establishes strong hands-on ML experience, including personally building models, but the most recent role is CSO. Shopify explicitly expects a live pair-programming interview. The candidate should therefore demonstrate that their Python/software-engineering skills remain strong enough for that bar. Shopify explicitly describes this interview requirement. ([Shopify][1])

3. **Agents specifically.**
   There is strong LLM/RAG/conversational-agent experience, but relatively little evidence of **production autonomous agent systems** specifically. That's a gap worth probing, although the JD doesn't appear to require years of agent-specific experience.

4. **Scale of people management.**
   "Extensive experience managing teams" is satisfied, but this isn't obviously someone who has managed a 30–50+ person ML organization. If Shopify means a relatively small, highly technical ML team, this is much less of a concern.

### Hiring-manager conclusion

**Strong candidate.**

I'd advance this resume to the interview stage. The strongest signal is not simply "15+ years of ML"; it's the combination of **ML management + marketplace/e-commerce + search/retrieval + recommendations/personalization + production LLMs/multimodal models + measurable business impact**.

The candidate looks particularly well aligned to the intersection Shopify describes: **large-scale ML applied to commerce, with search/recommendations and increasingly LLM-based experiences**. ([Shopify][1])

My biggest interview question would be: **"Can this person operate as an Engineering Manager at Shopify's pace and technical bar, rather than primarily as a senior applied scientist/technical leader?"** If the answer is yes, the resume supports a very strong case.
